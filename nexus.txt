Allows you to toggle off game sounds, all VC, or non-friends VC using menu toggles and keybinds. Includes hot mic detection with auto-mute and chat notifications.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]Sound[/b]
- Mute Master Sound
- Toggle Master Sound (Hotkey)
- Include VC in Master Mute
- Mute Music
- Toggle Music (Hotkey)
- Mute Sound Effects
- Toggle Sound Effects (Hotkey)
- Show Chat Notifications

[b]Voice Chat[/b]
- Mute All VC
- Toggle All VC (Hotkey)
- Mute Non-Friends VC
- Toggle Non-Friends VC (Hotkey)
- Mute Console Players VC
- Toggle Console Players VC (Hotkey)
- Ignore Friends
- Show Chat Notifications

[b]Hot Mic[/b]
- Auto-Mute Hot Mics
- Transmission Threshold (Seconds)
- Ignore Friends
- Show Chat Notifications
- Reset Hot Mic Mutes (Hotkey)

[b]Chat Commands:[/b]
- [b]/mute[/b] or [b]/mute all[/b] - Toggle master sound
- [b]/mute music[/b] - Toggle music
- [b]/mute sfx[/b] - Toggle sound effects
- [b]/mute vc[/b] - Toggle all voice chat
- [b]/mute nonfriends[/b] - Toggle non-friends voice chat
- [b]/mute console[/b] - Toggle console players voice chat
- [b]/mute reset[/b] - Reset hot mic auto-mutes
- [b]/mute help[/b] - Show available commands
(Note: /unmute can also be used)
